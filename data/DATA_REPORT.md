### Additional domain-shift factors (the project spec section 4.3)

- **Norway's images have vehicles masked out**, as documented in the RDD2022 data article
  (Arya et al., *Geoscience Data Journal*, 2024). This is a further domain-shift factor
  beyond resolution: the masking alters image statistics in a way no other country's
  subset shares. Record it when analysing Norway's zero-shot numbers in Phase 4.
- **China ships as two subsets with different capture modalities** — `China_Drone`
  (aerial) and `China_MotorBike` (vehicle-mounted). Drone imagery is a genuinely
  different domain from vehicle-mounted, so the project spec section 4.3 requires treating and
  reporting them separately rather than merging blindly. **See the open item in section 8
  below — the current build merges them.**

## 8. RESOLVED — China is split, not merged

`data/processed/target/` contains **`china_drone`** (2,401 images, aerial capture) and
**`china_motorbike`** (1,977 images, vehicle-mounted capture) as two separate zero-shot
target groups, each with its own `config/dataset_target_china_*.yaml`. This follows
the project spec section 4.3 ("China appears as two subsets with different capture modalities...
treat and report them separately, do not merge blindly") and section 5.2 (never average
away per-country variation — "the variation between countries is itself a result").
Decision confirmed by the student; previously flagged here as an open conflict against
section 5.1/6's single "china" entry, now settled in favor of section 4.3's explicit
instruction.
